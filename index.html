About

I am a Research Assistant at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Dhaka, advised by Md. Fahim Arefin. I recently completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering and am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

My research sits at the intersection of AI for Software Engineering and AI for Education — specifically, how large language models reason, generate, and evaluate code in real-world and competitive settings. I am particularly interested in building evaluation frameworks that go beyond execution metrics to capture genuine behavioral adequacy.
